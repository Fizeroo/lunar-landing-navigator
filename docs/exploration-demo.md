# Exploration demo

## One-minute live walkthrough

**0–15 seconds — compare options.** Open the app. A/B start at illustrative south-polar feature points. Show the common UTC period, Sun/Earth/joint bars and metric-specific conclusions. Open Scenarios: A now, A three days later, and B now use equal seven-day windows. The leading scenario depends on the metric; none is certified safe for landing.

**15–25 seconds — find the next change.** Close Scenarios and select Next Earthrise or Next joint window. Time, marker, light and both local skies change together. If no event is found, explain that the search is bounded by the prepared October2026–February2027 data.

**25–35 seconds — orient in space.** Switch to Space view. Identify the Moon, Earth and Sun. Directions are ephemeris-derived; distances and sizes are explicitly not to scale.

**35–50 seconds — descend and look.** Select Explore surface, then Look at Sun and Look at Earth. Drag to look around; read the compass and the true below-horizon/occlusion labels. Change observer height and distinguish the reference plane from the dipped sphere limb. The display camera has a minimum clearance separate from scientific height.

**50–60 seconds — inspect real terrain.** Choose Explore real LOLA terrain. This is one real 80m-grid patch, at true vertical scale. Look at a locally blocked body and open the local horizon profile. Move time/height. Explain that absence of a local blocker does not certify a clear horizon beyond this small DEM; A/B/event results remain reference-sphere comparisons.
